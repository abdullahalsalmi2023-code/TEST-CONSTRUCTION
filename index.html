About Northspire

A group built on
structural honesty.

Northspire is a full-service construction group delivering commercial, residential and civic projects with uncompromising attention to detail. Our approach pairs traditional craft with contemporary engineering — buildings that are honest in their materials and generous in their longevity.

From feasibility and pre-construction through delivery and aftercare, a single accountable team carries each project. We believe the best buildings come from early collaboration, disciplined budgets, and an obsessive respect for the people who will use them.

Every engagement is led by a senior director — no hand-offs, no dilution of intent.

Construction Process

From first line
to final handover.

  1. 01

    Discover & Define

    Feasibility studies, site analysis and brief development. We pressure-test ambition against budget before a single drawing is committed.

  2. 02

    Design & Engineer

    Coordinated design in a full digital twin — structure, services and facade resolved in 3D before ground is broken.

  3. 03

    Plan & Procure

    Transparent procurement, long-lead ordering and a construction programme sequenced to the day.

  4. 04

    Build & Verify

    Disciplined site operations with continuous quality inspection, live progress reporting and zero-compromise safety.

  5. 05

    Handover & Aftercare

    Commissioning, training and a 24-month aftercare programme. We stay accountable long after the ribbon is cut.

Sustainability

Building for the
next hundred years.

Low-carbon materials

Mass timber, recycled steel and low-clinker concrete specified as the default — not the exception. Embodied carbon reported on every bid.

Zero-waste sites

Segregated waste streams and take-back agreements with suppliers. Our current diversion-from-landfill rate stands above 96%.

Energy-positive design

Passive-first engineering, on-site generation and building fabric tuned to its climate — designed to give back more than it takes.
